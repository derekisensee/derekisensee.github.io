Hole Lotta Balls

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026.

This privacy policy explains how Hole Lotta Balls handles information in the iPhone app.

Information We Collect

Hole Lotta Balls does not require an account and does not ask for your email address, phone number, or other contact details. The app does not access your photos, contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, Bluetooth, calendars, reminders, or health data. The app does not use third-party analytics SDKs.

The app sends limited leaderboard and gameplay information to our own server, uses Google AdMob for ads, and offers an optional purchase through Apple, as described below.

Local Game Data

Hole Lotta Balls stores game progress on your device: unlocked levels, stars, coins, best scores, owned and equipped skins, your leaderboard name, and a random player ID.

Local game data is used only to make the app work and restore your progress. If you delete the app, iOS may remove this data.

Online Leaderboard

When you complete a level, the app sends the level number, your score, your leaderboard name, and a random player ID to our leaderboard server, which runs on Cloudflare. The player ID is generated on your device and is not linked to your Apple ID, device identifiers, or contact details. The app starts you with a random name, and you can change it at any time. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as your leaderboard name.

Your best score for each level is stored with your player ID and name, and your name and score are shown publicly to other players on that level's leaderboard. Cloudflare processes network information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure these requests, under Cloudflare's policies.

To have your leaderboard entries removed, open a support issue with your leaderboard name and the levels involved.

Cloudflare privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

Gameplay Analytics

To find levels that are too hard, fix problems, and decide how often ads appear, the app sends anonymous gameplay events to our server on Cloudflare: for example level started, won or lost (with score and time), goal reached, ad shown or rewarded (and where in the game), daily reward claimed, skin bought or equipped, and purchase completed. Each event is tied only to the same random player ID used for the leaderboard, a random per-launch session ID, and the app version.

These events do not include your name, contact details, device identifiers, or location, and are not shared with or sold to anyone. They are deleted automatically after 60 days.

Purchases

Hole Lotta Balls offers an optional Remove Ads in-app purchase through the Apple App Store. Apple handles all payment information; we never see it. The app stores whether ads have been removed so it can hide ads between levels and restore the purchase.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules at most one local reminder a day on your device when a new daily challenge is available. No notification data leaves your device.

Advertising

Hole Lotta Balls uses Google AdMob to show interstitial ads between levels and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus.

AdMob and Google's consent tools may process advertising data, ad interactions, approximate location, device and app information, diagnostics, and identifiers used by Google ad services. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows Google's consent form before ads are requested.

Tracking And Identifiers

Hole Lotta Balls shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you do not allow it, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads may be less personalized. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Hole Lotta Balls does not sell personal information.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

Hole Lotta Balls is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Support Requests

If you choose to contact support or open a GitHub issue, the information you provide there may be visible on GitHub depending on how you submit it. Please avoid including private personal information in public support requests.

Support page: Hole Lotta Balls Support

Changes To This Policy

If Hole Lotta Balls changes how it handles data, analytics, tracking, or advertising, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.
