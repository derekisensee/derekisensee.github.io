Support

Hole Lotta Balls

Help for the iPhone game Hole Lotta Balls: drag a hole, swallow the balls, and grow big enough before the timer runs out.

Need Help?

If something is broken, confusing, or missing, please open an issue on GitHub and include your device, iOS version, the level number, and a short description of what happened.

Support and bug reports: github.com/derekisensee/derekisensee.github.io/issues

Common Questions

  • Red balls shrink your hole and cost two seconds. Avoid them.
  • If a ball gets stuck in your hole, shake the hole back and forth hard to pop it through.
  • Reaching the goal size starts overtime: keep eating until the clock runs out for a higher score.
  • Progress, coins, and skins are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
  • Online high scores need an internet connection. Your best score is still saved on the device when offline.
  • You can change the name shown on the leaderboard from the level complete screen.
